Common questions

How many sheets of drywall do I need for a 12x12 room?

A 12×12 ft room with 8 ft ceilings has about 320 sq ft of wall area before doors and windows. At standard 4×8 ft sheets (32 sq ft each) with 10% waste, that's roughly 11 sheets for the walls, or about 16 if you're also sheeting the ceiling. Use the calculator above with your exact numbers for a precise figure.

What size drywall sheets should I use?

4×8 ft sheets are the standard — easiest to handle alone and they fit through most doorways. Larger 4×12 sheets mean fewer seams to tape and finish, which is faster on big open walls, but they're heavier and harder to maneuver solo.

How much waste should I allow for drywall?

10% is standard for a straightforward rectangular room. Rooms with lots of angles, alcoves, or a high number of windows and doors relative to wall area can need 15% or more.

Do I need drywall for the ceiling too?

Only if you're finishing an open ceiling, like in new construction or a gutted room. Most renovations that keep the existing ceiling only need wall sheets. Check the box above if your ceiling needs new drywall too.
