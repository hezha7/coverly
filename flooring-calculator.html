Common questions

How much flooring do I need for a 12x12 room?

A 12×12 ft room has 144 sq ft of floor area. Adding the standard 10% waste allowance for cuts brings that to about 158 sq ft. At 20 sq ft per box, that's 8 boxes. Use the calculator above with your exact numbers for a precise figure.

How much extra flooring should I buy for waste?

10% is the standard allowance for a simple rectangular room with a standard straight layout. Bump that up to 15–20% for diagonal layouts, herringbone patterns, or rooms with lots of angles and cuts, since more material gets trimmed away and wasted.

How many boxes of flooring do I need?

Divide your total area — including the waste allowance — by the coverage per box listed on the product, then round up to the next full box. Box coverage varies a lot by product, commonly 15–30 sq ft per box, so check your specific flooring's packaging and adjust the "box coverage" field above.

Should I order extra flooring for future repairs?

Yes — it's common practice to keep at least one full extra box after installation. Flooring runs and colors get discontinued over time, so matching a repair years later can be difficult or impossible without a leftover box from the original run.
